# Pricing review — round 2

Regenerated 13 Aug 2026 after the family-key fix, which merged five Sufix 832
spools that were previously invisible to the grouping. **No prices changed.**

## What to do with this

Two separate questions, and they deserve different answers.

**1. Colour scatter within a size.** The same flasher at $22.99 in one colour
and $25.99 in another is the one a shopper actually sees, because the sibling
module now lists them side by side. Every price traces to a real audited
street price, so the spread is genuine market variation — but "genuine" and
"looks deliberate" are different things. `proposed` is the group median.

**2. Odd cents endings.** $34.84, $18.69, $93.15, $164.94. Each is a real
retailer price we matched. Rounding them to .99 either gives up margin or
quietly charges above the market. There is no free version of this.

## Ignore these — not true siblings

`(no spec)` groups where the variants differ by *size* rather than colour are
correctly priced differently: Attwood Fold-N-Stow **Small vs Medium**, the
Attwood rod holder **0° vs 30°**, the Rapala tool holder **two-place vs
three-place**. Only act on rows where the sole difference is colour.
