# Burch Fishing Tackle — dealer application

**Apply:** https://burchfishingtackle.com → **Become a Dealer** (top nav)

Burch is Florence, Alabama. 15,000 SKUs, 60+ real tackle brands, and a
published blind-dropship program: *"Burch Tackle provides blind drop shipping
services for any online merchant or Dealer."* Sellers may use their own site,
Amazon, eBay or other platforms.

**They publish none of their terms.** No fees, no minimums, no shipping rates,
no image policy on the public site. You are applying blind and they tell you
afterwards. That is normal for them and not a red flag — but it means the
questions in §3 matter more than usual.

---

## 1. Why Burch, given we already have CWR

CWR stocks **zero fishing rods, two reels, and no fishing hooks**. See
SOURCING-REALITY.md. Burch is the tackle catalog — rods, reels, terminal
tackle, soft plastics, and the mainstream brands CWR either doesn't carry or
gates behind manufacturer authorisation.

Target split:

- **Burch** → `surf-rod`, `inshore-combo`, `circle-hooks`, `carolina-kit`, `swimbait-kit`, `braided-line`, `fluoro-leader`, `jig-assort`
- **CWR** → `landing-net`, `pliers`, `tackle-bag`, `cooler`, `sand-spike` (rod holders), plus marine accessories

## 3. Ask these before you place an order

Their terms aren't published, so this is the substance of the application.
Put them in the notes field or in a follow-up email.

1. **Dropship fee** — per order, and is it separate from shipping?
2. **Shipping cost** — flat rate or weight-based? What does a single rod cost
   to send to California? (This is the number that decides whether rods are
   sellable at all — see the freight maths in SOURCING-REALITY.md §3.)
3. **Minimum order** — per order and per year?
4. **Blind shipping** — confirm the packing slip and return address show
   THEANGLERSTORE, not Burch.
5. **Dispatch time** — same-day cutoff? Which days do they ship?
6. **Product images and data** — is there a downloadable feed or image
   library, and are we licensed to use the photography on our site? *(CWR's
   self-serve export is the benchmark. If Burch has nothing comparable, that's
   a real cost — it means no product photos for the rod and reel half of the
   catalog.)*
7. **Brand authorisation** — which brands need separate manufacturer approval?
   CWR gates Abu Garcia, Berkley, Daiwa, PENN, Pflueger. Does Burch?
8. **Stock feed** — how do we know something is in stock before we sell it?

---

## 4. What "good" looks like in their answer

Judge the account on these, in order:

- **Shipping on a 7-foot rod to California under ~$25.** Rods are long-parcel
  freight and this is the make-or-break number for `surf-rod` and
  `inshore-combo`. If it's $45, those two products don't work from Alabama at
  any honest retail price and we rethink.
- **Licensed images.** Without them the rod half of the catalog stays
  illustrated, and Search Console keeps flagging missing `image`.
- **A stock feed.** Selling something Burch doesn't have is worse than not
  listing it.
- **No minimum order.** Anything else is inventory risk you can't carry yet.

Everything else is negotiable.
