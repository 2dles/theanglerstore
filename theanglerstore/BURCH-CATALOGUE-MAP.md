# The Burch catalogue, mapped

Pulled 23 Sep 2026 from Burch's own product feed, every page, deduplicated.
**13,778 products, 7,423 of them in stock.** Prices in the feed are our dealer
cost, confirmed by matching "Daiwa FT Surf Spinning Rod 10' 2pc M" at $22.33
against the $22.33 already recorded in `supplier.ts`.

This exists because the catalogue was built without it. Earlier sourcing notes
sampled about 30 of Burch's 204 product types and drew conclusions from that.

---

## 1. The number that explains the storefront

```
Our 245 products:
  CWR   (boat outfitter)   189    77%
  Burch (tackle house)      56    23%
```

Burch has 7,423 items in stock and we list 56 of them. CWR has no Rods
category, no Hooks category and no Sinkers category anywhere in its
navigation, and it supplies three quarters of our shelf.

That is the whole problem. Not supply, not brand authorisation, not the
terminal tackle gap. We shopped at the boat store.

## 6. What the catalogue should look like

In the order a shore angler actually buys, which is roughly the inverse of
what we have today:

| Priority | Target depth | Have now |
|---|---|---|
| Terminal tackle: hooks, weights, swivels, slides, rigs | 40 to 50 | 15 |
| Line and leader: braid, mono, fluoro | 20 to 25 real products | ~4 distinct |
| Lures and bait a shore angler can cast | 30 to 40 | ~8 |
| Rods, reels, combos | 20 to 25 | 16 |
| Tools, nets, storage, apparel | 30 to 40 | 47 |
| Boat and trolling | keep, rank last | 56 |

The only category that needs nothing is the one we went deepest on.

Next step is a buy list against these targets, priced and margin-checked
before anything is written to `products.ts`.
